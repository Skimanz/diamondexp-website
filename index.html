New Zealand race coaching

Ash Johns — Interfield Coach | QAST

We’re excited to welcome Ash Johns back to QAST for his second season as an Interfield Coach. Last year, he made a great impact with our youngest athletes, helping them build confidence, improve their skiing, and develop a love for the sport.

Ash has been involved in ski racing from a young age, competing in New Zealand, Italy, and the United States. While completing a Bachelor of Management Studies at Waikato University, he raced for four years with the Waikato Ski Team.

He brings extensive international coaching experience from North America, Japan, and New Zealand, including Jackson Hole, Whistler, and now Niseko, Japan, where he is based for the Northern Hemisphere winters.

Ash is committed to continually developing himself as both a coach and athlete, always looking for ways to refine his technical understanding, communication, and impact. Through ongoing learning, reflection, and time on snow, he aims to stay adaptable, relevant, and effective—bringing fresh ideas, greater clarity, and high-quality experiences to the athletes he works with.

Off the snow, Ash enjoys travelling to new places, mountain biking, scuba diving, and hiking. We’re thrilled to have him back with QAST for the 2026 season.
