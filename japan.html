Why ski Japan?

Come for the snow. Stay for the experience. Japan combines remarkably consistent winter snow with welcoming culture, memorable food and mountains that offer something for every skier.

01

Consistent snowfall

Regular storms can refresh the mountains throughout your stay, creating the light snow Japan is famous for.

02

Terrain for every level

Enjoy sheltered tree skiing, open runs and progressive terrain—from first powder turns to challenging mountain days.

03

More than one mountain

Niseko is only the beginning. Rusutsu, Kiroro, Sapporo Kokusai and Teine each offer a different experience.

04

Culture beyond skiing

Onsens, exceptional food and Japanese hospitality make the time away from the slopes just as memorable.

If you cancel

Send your cancellation in your booking WhatsApp conversation or using the contact details on your invoice. The time your message is received determines the refund. All deadlines use Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9), measured back from the scheduled start of each affected lesson.

  • More than 14 days before: 90% of the affected lesson price is refunded; 10% is retained as a cancellation charge.
  • From 7 days up to and including 14 days before: 50% is refunded.
  • Less than 7 days before, or a no-show: no refund for a guest cancellation, subject to applicable law.

For bookings with multiple lessons, each lesson is assessed separately. A day means 24 hours: exactly 14 days and exactly 7 days before the scheduled start both fall in the 50% refund band. These percentages include the cancellation charge; no additional payment-processing deduction is taken from the stated refund. Refunds are calculated in JPY. Your bank or payment provider may apply its own currency conversion rates or charges.

Changes, weather and cancellations by Ash

Ask Ash about date, resort or participant changes as early as possible. Changes depend on availability and must be agreed in writing. Requesting a change does not itself amend or cancel your booking.

Lessons may proceed in normal winter weather where safe and suitable terrain is available. If Ash cancels, or resort closures or unsafe conditions prevent the booked service from being delivered, you may choose an agreed alternative or a full refund for the undelivered service. The guest-cancellation charges above do not apply in those circumstances. A change of resort or material change to the service will be agreed with you first.
